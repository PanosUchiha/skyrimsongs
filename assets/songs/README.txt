Put MIDI files (.mid) in this folder and they show up in the songbook (Tab while you play).

Each song can be:
  - Played along with: the tune (the part that looks most like a melody, one note at a time) slides across the
    screen with the key to press for each note, and you're judged on every note.
  - Let play: the whole file plays itself on your instrument, perfectly. The crowd won't know.

Drum parts (MIDI channel 10) are left out.
